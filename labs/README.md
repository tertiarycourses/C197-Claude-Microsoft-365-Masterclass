# Labs — Claude Microsoft 365 Masterclass (C197)

**Course Code:** C197  |  **Version v2.0 · 12 August 2026**

All 11 activities use one fictional company, **Lumina Living Pte Ltd**, and build a connected FY2027 planning and management pack.

The presentation explains concepts, decisions, process maps and realistic work samples. These lab folders and the Learner Guide contain the complete prompts, commands and verification checks.

## Lab folder standard

Every lab folder contains a detailed README, a realistic company Word brief, an Excel working file, an editable PowerPoint starter and reusable review/approval templates. Lab 11 also contains safe local automation starters.

## Lab sequence

| Topic | Lab | Activity | Company outcome |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 01 | 01 | [Choose and Activate the Right Claude Surface](lab-01-choose-and-activate-the-right-claude-surface/README.md) | A completed operating-surface decision matrix and environment-readiness record. |
| 01 | 02 | [Connect Microsoft 365 Context and Map Permissions](lab-02-connect-microsoft-365-context-and-permissions/README.md) | A least-privilege source register with access status, evidence owner, retention note and approved use. |
| 01 | 03 | [Build an Auditable Prompt and Review Contract](lab-03-build-an-auditable-prompt-and-review-contract/README.md) | A reusable cross-app prompt library, prompt test log and acceptance checklist. |
| 02 | 04 | [Create a Company Marketing Plan in Word](lab-04-create-a-company-marketing-plan-in-word/README.md) | A reviewed FY2027 marketing plan in Word with an evidence appendix and management decision page. |
| 02 | 05 | [Develop a Strategic Plan with Owners and Measures](lab-05-develop-a-strategic-plan-with-owners-and-measures/README.md) | A three-year strategic plan, initiative portfolio and quarterly review scorecard in Word and Excel. |
| 02 | 06 | [Draft Sustainability Reporting and HR Policy](lab-06-draft-sustainability-reporting-and-hr-policy/README.md) | A sustainability performance section, metric register and flexible-work policy draft with review and approval records. |
| 03 | 07 | [Build Financial Analysis and an Excel Dashboard](lab-07-build-financial-analysis-and-an-excel-dashboard/README.md) | A controlled FY2026 financial workbook with actual-vs-budget analysis, scenarios, three native charts and an executive dashboard. |
| 03 | 08 | [Create an Executive Strategy and Marketing Deck](lab-08-create-an-executive-strategy-and-marketing-deck/README.md) | A ten-slide strategic and marketing presentation with native Excel charts, varied layouts and an executive decision page. |
| 04 | 09 | [Triage and Prepare Approved Outlook Replies](lab-09-triage-and-prepare-approved-outlook-replies/README.md) | A triage queue, cited thread summary, approved reply templates, draft responses and an unsent meeting invitation. |
| 04 | 10 | [Coordinate the Planning Pack with Claude Cowork](lab-10-coordinate-the-planning-pack-with-claude-cowork/README.md) | A Cowork project folder, execution plan, consolidated management brief, discrepancy log and reviewed Office hand-off. |
| 04 | 11 | [Automate Excel, Outlook and a Daily Brief with Claude Code](lab-11-automate-excel-outlook-and-a-daily-brief-with-claude-code/README.md) | A reviewed automation plan, local workbook-update script, connector-assisted Outlook search and generated daily brief with run log. |
