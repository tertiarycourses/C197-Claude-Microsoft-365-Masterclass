# Lab 1 — Get Started with Claude for Microsoft 365

**Topic 01:** Getting Started with Claude for Microsoft 365  |  **Day 1**  |  **Approx. 20 min**  |  **Course:** Claude Microsoft 365 Masterclass (C197)

## Scenario

Lumina Living is a small home-and-lifestyle retailer. The quarter has just closed and your manager needs the Q3 business-review pack — a short written report, the numbers behind it, a slide deck for the management meeting, and the emails that send it out — by the end of the day. You have a rough brief, a sales workbook and a handful of facts to work from. Across this course you use Claude alongside Microsoft 365 to turn that raw material into a finished, checked pack. Use this scenario only if you cannot use real, non-confidential work of your own; your own material is always preferred.

## Goal

Sign in to Claude, understand how it works alongside Microsoft 365, and run a first prompt on your task.

## What you'll build

A working Claude account with your first, verified answer — a plain-language summary of the Q3 task and a checklist of what the review pack needs.

**Tools and techniques:** claude.ai (or the Claude desktop app), Microsoft 365, a web browser

## Prerequisites

- A Claude account at claude.ai (free or paid) — or the Claude desktop app installed and signed in.
- Microsoft 365 with Word available, so you can open the sample brief.
- Chrome or Edge, signed in to the correct account.

## Steps

### Step 1

Sign in at claude.ai (or open the Claude desktop app) and click 'New chat'. This is where you will work with Claude all day.

### Step 2

Open the sample 'Lumina Living — Q3 Review Brief' (a short Word document the trainer shares), or use a short brief of your own non-confidential work.

### Step 3

Copy the brief's text and paste it into the Claude chat with this instruction so Claude tells you what the task involves.

Prompt to give Claude (paste into the chat):

```text
Here is the brief for a quarterly business review. In plain language, summarise what I am being asked to produce, then list the separate items the final pack should contain.
```

### Step 4

Read Claude's answer. Check it against the brief: does its list match what the brief actually asks for? Add anything it missed.

### Step 5

Ask one follow-up so you see Claude reason over your material.

Prompt to give Claude (paste into the chat):

```text
For each item in that list, say which Microsoft 365 app I would use to produce it — Word, Excel, PowerPoint or Outlook.
```

### Step 6

Confirm the ground rule: Claude has only read text you gave it, and nothing has changed in your files. Claude drafts; you decide what to keep.

### Step 7

Save this chat — rename it 'Lumina Living Q3'. You now have Claude set up and a clear, checked picture of the task.

## Test it

You are signed in to Claude, and you have a first answer — a summary of the Q3 task and a checklist of the pack's items — that you have checked against the brief and corrected where needed.

## Troubleshooting

- **You can't sign in to claude.ai.** Confirm you are using the email you registered with, and try a password reset from the sign-in page. If a verification email does not arrive, check spam and wait a minute before resending. In the desktop app, sign out and back in so it picks up your session.
- **The pasted brief doesn't appear in full.** Pasting a very long document can truncate in the message box — send it in two parts, or save the brief as a Word or PDF file and attach it with the paperclip instead (you will do exactly this in Lab 2).
- **Claude's summary misses an item the brief clearly asks for.** Do not just accept it — point Claude back at the source: 'The brief also mentions the emails that send the pack out. Re-read it and complete the list.' Then compare its revised list against the brief line by line.

## Challenge

Ask Claude to turn its checklist into a short numbered plan for the day, in the order you should tackle the items — report, numbers, slides, emails. Save that plan in the same chat; it becomes your roadmap through the connected Lumina Living Q3 pack.

## Reflection

LO1 — Sign in to Claude, understand how it works alongside Microsoft 365, and run a first prompt on your task. In your own words, how will you use this in your own work, and how will you check Claude got it right?

## Deliverable

Save your work — this checked task summary and checklist become part of the connected **Lumina Living Q3 review pack**, the single deliverable you complete and send in Lab 8.
