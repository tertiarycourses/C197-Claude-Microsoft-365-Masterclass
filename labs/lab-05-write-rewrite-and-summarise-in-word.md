# Lab 5 — Write, Rewrite and Summarise in Word

**Topic 02:** Boosting Productivity Across Microsoft 365 with Claude  |  **Day 1**  |  **Approx. 40 min**  |  **Course:** Claude Microsoft 365 Masterclass (C197)

## Scenario

Lumina Living is a small home-and-lifestyle retailer. The quarter has just closed and your manager needs the Q3 business-review pack — a short written report, the numbers behind it, a slide deck for the management meeting, and the emails that send it out — by the end of the day. You have a rough brief, a sales workbook and a handful of facts to work from. Across this course you use Claude alongside Microsoft 365 to turn that raw material into a finished, checked pack. Use this scenario only if you cannot use real, non-confidential work of your own; your own material is always preferred.

## Goal

Use Claude to draft, restructure and tighten a document, and to summarise a long one, then finish it in Word.

## What you'll build

A finished Q3 review report in Microsoft Word, drafted and refined with Claude and checked by you.

**Tools and techniques:** Microsoft Word, Claude (draft / rewrite / summarise), copy-paste, Word > Home formatting

## Prerequisites

- Labs 1–4 completed: Claude is set up, your Q3 brief and sales workbook are connected in your Project, you have a reusable prompt pattern, and you have written your safe-use checklist.
- Microsoft Word installed and open (desktop or web).
- Remember: Claude is a separate assistant at claude.ai or in the Claude desktop app — it has no ribbon inside Word. You draft in Claude, then copy the checked text into Word yourself.

## Steps

### Step 1

In your Project, ask Claude to draft the report from the material it already has.

Prompt to give Claude (paste into the chat):

```text
Using the brief and the Q3 workbook in this project, draft a one-page business-review report for management. Structure it as: Overview, What sold well, What to watch, and Recommended actions. Name the key figure in each section and say where it comes from.
```

### Step 2

Read the draft and check every figure it names against the workbook. Mark anything you cannot confirm — you will not keep unverified numbers.

### Step 3

Restructure and tighten with a follow-up prompt.

Prompt to give Claude (paste into the chat):

```text
Good. Now cut it to about 250 words, put the Recommended actions as three bullet points, and make the tone confident but plain — no jargon.
```

### Step 4

Adjust the tone for the audience if needed (for example more formal, or warmer), then choose the version you will keep.

Prompt to give Claude (paste into the chat):

```text
Give me the same report in a slightly more formal tone suitable for a board paper.
```

### Step 5

Summarise a longer input: paste a longer background note (or the brief's appendix) and ask for a short summary you can use as an intro.

Prompt to give Claude (paste into the chat):

```text
Summarise this background note into four bullet points I can use as context at the top of the report.
```

### Step 6

Open Microsoft Word, paste your chosen report in, and format it: a title, headings for each section, and the three action bullets (Home > Styles).

### Step 7

Do the final human check: re-read the whole page, confirm every figure ties to the workbook, and fix any wording. Save it as 'Lumina Living — Q3 Review Report.docx'.

## Test it

You have a saved Word report of about 250 words with the four sections and three action bullets, in which every figure has been checked against the Q3 workbook.

## Troubleshooting

- **Claude names a figure you cannot find in the workbook.** Do not keep it. Ask Claude to say exactly which column and rows it used; open the Q3 workbook and confirm the number yourself. If it still does not tie out, reject the figure and re-ask, naming the column you want it drawn from — never let an unverified number reach the report.
- **Pasting into Word drags in odd fonts, colours or spacing.** Paste as text instead — in Word use Home > Paste > Keep Text Only (or Ctrl+Shift+V) — then apply your own Word Styles for the title, headings and bullets so the document matches your house look.
- **The draft is much longer than one page.** Ask Claude to cut to a hard word count ('reduce to 250 words, keep the four sections') rather than 'make it shorter'; a named number gives you a page you can actually use, and you can trim the least important 'What to watch' detail last.

## Challenge

Ask Claude to produce a two-sentence executive summary of your finished report that you can place at the very top, then verify that its headline figure matches the report exactly before you add it to the Lumina Living Q3 pack.
